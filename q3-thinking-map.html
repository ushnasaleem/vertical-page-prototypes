The premise — why we're doing any of this

RPO & BPO buyers are squeezed on margin. Beam's whole pitch is operating leverage — the same team handling far more volume. So we lead with the margin story and narrow to RPO (CV-screening) first.

But today the only way in is "book a demo" — too steep for someone not ready. The fix: value-based off-ramps (a white paper, an ROI calculator) that give something useful before we ask for the meeting, and earn trust on the way.

Can two people do this?

Short answer: a 2-person team can ship the spine — white paper, Booth case study, calculator, nurture, lean paid. It cannot also run the full channel plan at full cadence. With no extra support, keep the compounding lines, cut the volume + experiments, and buy time with freelance production.

✓ Protect — the critical path

  • White paper — the fast flagship (process discovery; Harris drafts it)
  • Booth case study + Hudson clearance — unlocks ABM
  • ROI calculator — in-house dev builds it; best converter
  • Email nurture — already built, fast, high ROI
  • Paid restructure + SEO/AEO quick fixes — cheap, high value

✕ Cut / defer — if no extra support

  • Blog 8/mo → 2–4/mo, BOFU-only (organic isn't a Q3 lead source anyway)
  • Webinars → defer to Q4 (or one lean co-host)
  • Product Hunt one-time → cut (low-ICP audience)
  • New RPO reference → defer; Booth is enough anchor
  • Founder LinkedIn cadence → only if Jonas drives it
  • BPO expansion → already after the calculator (naturally later)
